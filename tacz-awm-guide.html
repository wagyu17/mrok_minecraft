Basic Controls

Input Action How to Use It
Left Click Fire Shoot while holding a loaded gun.
Right Click Aim / ADS Hold to aim down sights by default.
R Reload Reload when you have compatible ammunition.
G Fire Mode Switch between supported fire modes such as semi-auto and full-auto.
Z Customize Open the attachment and weapon customization screen.
H Inspect Play a weapon inspection animation.
V Melee / Zoom Melee when not aiming. Change zoom while aiming with a scope.
O Interact Interact with blocks or entities while holding a gun.

Quick Start

  1. Craft or obtain a gunsmith table.
  2. Create a gun, compatible ammunition, and attachments.
  3. Put the gun and matching ammunition in your inventory.
  4. Hold the gun and press R to reload.
  5. Hold right click to aim, then left click to fire.
  6. Press G to switch fire mode if the weapon supports it.
  7. Press Z to open the customization screen.

Common Beginner Problems

The gun does not fire

Check whether the gun is loaded and whether you are carrying compatible ammunition.

Right click opens aiming instead of a chest

When holding a gun, right click is used for ADS. Use O to interact with blocks or entities.

Zoom does not work

Press V while aiming down sights. If you are not aiming, V works as melee instead.

Alt + T does not open settings

The in-game settings screen requires Cloth Config API. Without it, TaCZ can show a prompt instead of opening the config screen.
